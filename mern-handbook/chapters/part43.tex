\chapter{Authentication Errors}

\begin{notebox}[NOTE]
These errors sit specifically in the login/session/OAuth path --- narrower
than the general backend errors in Chapter 42, but common enough to
deserve their own quick-reference.
\end{notebox}

\subsubsection*{401 Unauthorized}
\noindent\begin{tabularx}{\textwidth}{@{}>{\bfseries}p{2.3cm}X@{}}
\toprule
Cause & Request reached a protected route with no valid identity --- no
token, expired token, or invalid token. \\
Identify & Response status \texttt{401}; body typically says "Not
authorized" or "No token provided." \\
Solution & Confirm the user is logged in and the cookie/token is actually
being sent; re-authenticate if the session expired. \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabularx}

\subsubsection*{403 Forbidden}
\noindent\begin{tabularx}{\textwidth}{@{}>{\bfseries}p{2.3cm}X@{}}
\toprule
Cause & The user \emph{is} authenticated but lacks the role/permission
required for this action (e.g. non-admin hitting an admin route). \\
Identify & Response status \texttt{403}, distinct from \texttt{401} ---
identity is known, access is denied. \\
Solution & This is expected behavior for role-based routes; verify the
user's role matches what \texttt{authorize(...)} requires (Chapter 21). \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabularx}

\subsubsection*{JWT Not Found (Cookie Missing)}
\noindent\begin{tabularx}{\textwidth}{@{}>{\bfseries}p{2.3cm}X@{}}
\toprule
Cause & The auth cookie was never set, or the browser didn't send it back
with the request. \\
Identify & \texttt{req.cookies.token} is \texttt{undefined} in the
\texttt{protect} middleware. \\
Solution & Confirm login actually set the cookie (check DevTools
Application $\to$ Cookies) and that the frontend request includes
credentials (see below). \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabularx}

\subsubsection*{JWT Invalid}
\noindent\begin{tabularx}{\textwidth}{@{}>{\bfseries}p{2.3cm}X@{}}
\toprule
Cause & Same root cause as Chapter 42 --- secret mismatch or a tampered
token --- but surfaced specifically through the \texttt{protect}
middleware on a login-dependent route. \\
Identify & \texttt{jwt.verify()} throws inside \texttt{protect}; response
is \texttt{401} with "Invalid token." \\
Solution & Clear the bad cookie and force re-login; verify
\texttt{JWT\_SECRET} consistency across restarts/deploys. \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabularx}

\subsubsection*{JWT Expired}
\noindent\begin{tabularx}{\textwidth}{@{}>{\bfseries}p{2.3cm}X@{}}
\toprule
Cause & Token's lifetime (\texttt{expiresIn}) has elapsed since login. \\
Identify & \texttt{TokenExpiredError} in \texttt{protect}; user was
working fine, then suddenly gets \texttt{401} on every request. \\
Solution & Prompt re-login on \texttt{401}; the frontend's Axios
interceptor should redirect to \texttt{/login} when this happens
(Chapter 33). \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabularx}

\subsubsection*{Cookie Not Sent}
\noindent\begin{tabularx}{\textwidth}{@{}>{\bfseries}p{2.3cm}X@{}}
\toprule
Cause & Frontend Axios instance is missing \texttt{withCredentials: true},
or the backend's \texttt{cors()} is missing \texttt{credentials: true}
with a specific (non-wildcard) origin. \\
Identify & Cookie is visibly set in DevTools after login, but subsequent
requests don't include it --- \texttt{req.cookies} is empty on the
backend. \\
Solution & Set \texttt{withCredentials: true} on the Axios instance
\emph{and} \texttt{cors(\{ origin: FRONTEND\_URL, credentials: true \})}
on the backend --- both sides are required. \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabularx}

\subsubsection*{Google OAuth: redirect\_uri\_mismatch}
\noindent\begin{tabularx}{\textwidth}{@{}>{\bfseries}p{2.3cm}X@{}}
\toprule
Cause & The redirect URI sent in the OAuth request doesn't exactly match
one of the "Authorized redirect URIs" configured in the Google Cloud
Console (protocol, domain, port, or trailing slash differs). \\
Identify & Google's consent screen shows "Error 400:
redirect\_uri\_mismatch" instead of the login prompt. \\
Solution & Copy the exact callback URL your backend uses into the Google
Cloud Console credentials, matching scheme and port precisely. \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabularx}

\subsubsection*{Google OAuth: invalid\_client}
\noindent\begin{tabularx}{\textwidth}{@{}>{\bfseries}p{2.3cm}X@{}}
\toprule
Cause & Wrong or missing \texttt{GOOGLE\_CLIENT\_ID}/\texttt{GOOGLE\_CLIENT\_SECRET},
or credentials from the wrong Google Cloud project. \\
Identify & Google returns "Error 401: invalid\_client" before showing the
consent screen. \\
Solution & Re-copy the client ID/secret from the correct project into
\texttt{.env} and restart the backend. \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabularx}

\subsubsection*{UI Not Updating After Login}
\noindent\begin{tabularx}{\textwidth}{@{}>{\bfseries}p{2.3cm}X@{}}
\toprule
Cause & Login succeeded and the cookie was set, but the React
\texttt{AuthContext}'s \texttt{user} state was never refreshed, so
components still render as "logged out." \\
Identify & Network tab shows a successful login response, but the navbar/
protected routes still behave as unauthenticated. \\
Solution & After a successful login call, update context state directly
(or re-fetch \texttt{/auth/me}) instead of relying on a page reload
(Chapter 32). \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabularx}
